# DeskTidy website design boundary

Date: 2026-08-15

The current site is deliberately a small status surface, not a product funnel.
It has two content pages: the repository-status landing page and privacy page.

## Principles

- Lead with the local-deployment result and public-distribution boundary.
- Link to repository evidence; do not simulate product availability.
- Keep navigation, motion, and interaction minimal enough to remain legible
  with CSS disabled or reduced motion enabled.
- Do not add a waitlist, analytics, tracking, pricing, payment, installer, or
  download surface until the matching product and privacy gates exist.
- Every deployment must retain keyboard-visible links, semantic headings,
  sufficient contrast, responsive text, and static generation.

The historical animated demo and marketing components were removed from the
active source because they no longer matched the repository's current claim.
